# Product Specification

## North star

Opening the app answers “今日何をすればいいのか” immediately. Completing the prominent action in a few minutes advances the Instagram operation. Results return as one understandable next experiment.

## Primary user and job

A Japanese, mobile-first operator of “からだのじかん” serving women around their forties. The user wants consistent, trustworthy educational content without becoming a marketer, analyst, or video editor.

## Funnel and content policy

Reach → Profile Visit → Follow → Trust → DM → Reservation Inquiry → Booking. Initial mix is Growth 60%, Trust 20%, Lifestyle 10%, Conversion 10%; Reels 70%, Carousel 20%, Other 10%; four posts/week. These are proposals, never autonomous permanent rules.

## Brand contract

Calm, gentle, positive, trustworthy, approachable, understandable, and never pushy. Do not shame bodies, diagnose, guarantee, exaggerate before/after, promise treatment, or invent evidence. AI evaluates content quality and brand fit—not bodies.

## Phase 1 journey

1. Login or enter Demo Mode.
2. Today presents one recommended post and three evidence-based reasons.
3. Generate produces a typed Reels plan.
4. Review hook, scenes, shot list, caption, CTA, and safety result.
5. Edit/regenerate a selected section without losing versions.
6. Save as draft to Calendar and Content Bank.

## Phase 1 acceptance

- The primary CTA is visible without scrolling on a 390×844 viewport.
- Every page has a useful empty state and next action.
- Safety status has text/icon in addition to color.
- Demo and Real modes cannot be confused.
- A BLOCK plan cannot enter approval or scheduling.
- Existing versions remain retrievable after regeneration.

## Non-goals now

Real posting, full video editing, cold outbound DMs, medical advice, advanced CRM, microservices, or autonomous strategy changes.
